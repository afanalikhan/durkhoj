About Dur Khoj & Khoj AI

Chitral, PK

Dur Khoj (meaning "Discovering a Home") is a dedicated property platform built to solve real community fragmentation in Chitral. Instead of lost WhatsApp messages and unverified posts, it centralizes house sales, land plots, and rentals into one digital space.

🏠

Community First Discovery

Designed around the unique local geography—from Booni to Ayun, Mastuj, and Chitral Town. Owners list directly with zero middleman fees.

🤖

Khoj AI Assistant Concept

Integrated natural language guide built to help users query real database listings (e.g. "Find a 3 bedroom house in Booni") without inventing fake data.
